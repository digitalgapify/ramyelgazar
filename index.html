The first international professional sumo competition ever staged in the United Arab Emirates. Nine wrestlers from Japan, Mongolia and Egypt, thirty six bouts in a single day, hosted by the Department of Culture and Tourism Abu Dhabi in collaboration with USA Sumo. Ramy was the only Arab wrestler on the card, in a line up that included a former sekiwake and Emperor's Cup winner, a World Sumo Champion and a wrestler with eight hundred and twenty five career bouts.

Facts

Ramy Elgazar, answered

Who is Ramy Elgazar?

Ramy Elgazar is an Egyptian American sumo wrestler, performer and chef based in Los Angeles. He is a four time US Sumo Open heavyweight champion, a ten time national champion of Egypt, and the first Egyptian and first Arab to win the heavyweight title at the US Sumo Open.

What else is Ramy Elgazar known as?

He also appears as Ramy El-Gazar, Ramy El Gazar and Sumo Guy, and in Arabic as رامي الجزار (مصارع سومو مصري, an Egyptian sumo wrestler). All of them refer to the same person.

How many US Sumo Open titles has Ramy Elgazar won?

Four US Sumo Open heavyweight titles. The first came on 8 August 2015 at the Walter Pyramid in Long Beach, where he ended the eight year unbeaten streak of Byambajav Ulambayar, a four time World Sumo Champion.

Is Ramy Elgazar the first Arab to win the US Sumo Open?

Yes. He is the first Egyptian and the first Arab to win the heavyweight title at the US Sumo Open, the largest sumo tournament in the world outside Japan.

What is Ramy Elgazar's height and weight?

He is 1.93 m (6 ft 4 in) tall, with a peak competition weight of 260 kg.

Where is Ramy Elgazar from?

He grew up in El Mahalla El Kubra, a textile town in the Nile Delta, Egypt. He moved to Los Angeles in 2016 and is based there.

Is Ramy Elgazar the same person as Osunaarashi?

No. Osunaarashi (Abdelrahman Shalan) is a different Egyptian sumo wrestler who competed in Japan's professional ranks. Ramy Elgazar is an Egyptian American wrestler based in Los Angeles who won the US Sumo Open heavyweight title and works as a referee, performer and chef.

What sports did Ramy Elgazar compete in before sumo?

Greco Roman wrestling from the age of seven, then judo. He joined the Egyptian national judo team at fourteen and took the Arab Championship and an African bronze before turning seventeen. He has spent 34 years in combat sport.

What is Ramy Elgazar's role with the International Sumo Federation and USA Sumo?

He is an International Sumo Federation referee and officiated at the World Combat Games in Riyadh in 2023. He is a board member and assistant coach at USA Sumo and coach to the United States national sumo team.

Did Ramy Elgazar compete in the first professional sumo event in the UAE?

Yes. On 26 September 2026 he was the only Arab wrestler on the card at All Star Sumo at Space42 Arena in Abu Dhabi, the first international professional sumo competition held in the United Arab Emirates. It featured nine wrestlers and thirty six bouts and sold out.

How many live shows has Ramy Elgazar performed?

About 1,900 live shows worldwide since 2015, with a cumulative live audience of more than 9.5 million, across five continents.

How big is Ramy Elgazar's online audience?

Content he appears in has passed 1.5 billion views across 32 creator collaborations on 16 channels, including MrBeast, Nick DiGiovanni and Airrack. He has close to one million followers across platforms.

Has Ramy Elgazar appeared on television or in film?

Yes. His credits include Lip Sync Battle, Tengo Talento, Mucho Talento, The Project in Australia, Ozarks Live, and the films Hail Mary, The Grifter and The Wide West.
