Positioning

He builds the door
when there isn't one

Ramy Elgazar came up in a sport his country barely funded, in a weight class nobody in the region competed at, from a textile town in the Nile Delta. There was no pathway in front of him, so he made one. He paid for his own flight to the United States in 2015, and three weeks after landing he took the heavyweight title at the US Sumo Open from a champion who had not lost there in eight years.

That is the whole method. He enters rooms where he is the only Egyptian and the only Arab, and he arrives prepared rather than hopeful. Ten national titles. Four US Sumo Open crowns. An international referee licence. Nearly nineteen hundred shows on five continents. A kitchen of his own.

He is also a father and the person his family relies on, and that is not incidental to the story. Every decision in this timeline was taken with that responsibility already on his shoulders. He has never accepted a ceiling on what he is allowed to be.

Athlete. Referee. Performer. Cook. Provider. Each one added without putting down the one before it.

8 August 2015 · Walter Pyramid, Long Beach
He ended
an eight year
unbeaten
streak

Byambajav Ulambayar, four time World Sumo Champion, had taken the US Sumo Open heavyweight gold eight years running. In front of four thousand people, Ramy took it from him, then went on to the openweight silver in the same day.

1992 to 2026

The road

1992

El Mahalla El Kubra

Starts Greco Roman wrestling at the Ghazl El Mahalla club at the age of seven, under Captain Abdel Hay El Qost, later head coach of the Saudi national team. His father is a maintenance technician at the Misr Spinning and Weaving Company and a former bodybuilder. The whole house is built around sport.

1994

Judo, and two sports at once

Takes up judo under Captain Metwally El Nafrawy. For two years he trains both disciplines in the same hall, wrestling from eight in the morning until noon, sleeping in the shade of the club, then judo from three until seven.

1999

Egypt national judo team at fourteen

Competes at over 100 kg. Takes the Arab Championship and an African bronze before turning seventeen. He fights the Arab final with a broken rib.

2000

The Sydney cycle

French expert Christophe Gagliano backs him for Olympic selection. He is not chosen. Egypt returns from Sydney without a judo medal. He leaves the sport soon afterwards.

2006

Sumo finds him

Introduced to sumo by Captain Bakr El Sharnouby. He enters the Egyptian national championship at the first attempt and wins it, still a junior.

2007 to 2012

Away from the mat

Five years out of competition. He keeps training, works, and waits for a door to open in a sport his country barely funds.

2013

International breakthrough

Joins Egypt's national sumo squad, then the strongest Arab team in the sport. He loses his first event in Poland by applying judo instinct, returns the same year and takes gold plus the Strongest Wrestler Cup. He stages the first public sumo event ever held in Egypt, at El Sawy Culturewheel in Cairo.

2014

Gold in Budapest, loss in Taiwan

Takes gold in Hungary. At the World Championships in Taiwan his foot sinks into newly laid clay and he loses to Byamba of Mongolia. What the Mongolian says to him afterwards is the reason he goes to America.

2015

America, and the title

He funds the trip himself out of savings, help from both parents and the sale of his motorcycle. He lands in New York on 17 July and works a nightclub door the same evening. Three weeks later he takes heavyweight gold and openweight silver at the US Sumo Open. A ninety minute American television documentary follows him through it. His following goes from five hundred to over one hundred thousand in days.

2016 to 2018

Turning professional

Signs with USA Sumo and moves to Los Angeles, where he has been based since. Bronze in 2016, a second gold in 2017. Begins the touring circuit, including Sumo and Sushi at the PlayStation Theater in New York in 2018. His photographs are exhibited in a Tokyo metro gallery under the title Egyptian Gallery in Tokyo.

2019 to 2020

Touring and filming

Openweight bronze at the US Sumo Open and a run of brand and creator work.

2021

The heaviest collision

Heavyweight silver. On the way there he meets 512 pound Simeon at 488 pounds himself, the heaviest meeting in more than two thousand matches of US Sumo Open history. He throws him down.

2023

Riyadh, and the officials' table

Officiates as an International Sumo Federation referee at the World Combat Games in Riyadh, and begins organising in the region.

2026

Abu Dhabi

The only Arab wrestler on the card at the first international professional sumo competition held in the UAE. Nine wrestlers, thirty six bouts, sold out at Space42 Arena, and the start of a development project for the sport in the Gulf.

26 September 2026 · Space42 Arena, Abu Dhabi

All Star Sumo
comes to the Gulf

The first international professional sumo competition ever staged in the United Arab Emirates. Nine wrestlers from Japan, Mongolia and Egypt, thirty six bouts in a single day, hosted by the Department of Culture and Tourism Abu Dhabi in collaboration with USA Sumo. The event sold out before it opened.

Ramy was the only Arab wrestler on the card, in a line up that included a former sekiwake and Emperor's Cup winner, a World Sumo Champion and a wrestler with eight hundred and twenty five career bouts.

"Every time I step into the sumo ring, I feel that I am carrying more than my own name. I am carrying the Egyptian flag and representing millions of people across the Arab world."

The second craft

Sumo Guy

He started cooking at seven or eight years old, before sumo entered the picture, in a house where the protein came off the roof. It was never a career plan. It was the thing he loved, and he kept doing it through every year of training.

The obsession turned technical. Competing at the top of the heavyweight division on a diet he designed and paid for himself, he learned nutrition the way he learned groundwork, by study. He now works as a sports nutritionist alongside the sport.

His first food video took half a million views in two hours. Two of his largest collaborations are food rather than sport, Nick DiGiovanni's whole cow cook and unlimited sushi challenge, sixty eight million views between them, plus a campaign for OXO knives and salt.

Everything he puts out is his own. His recipes are developed in his kitchen rather than adapted from anyone else, and the spice blends behind them are built by him and used across the range, which is what gives the food a signature rather than a style.

Sogo', Eastern style Egyptian sausage

Handmade from premium cuts and his own spice blend, chasing what he calls the true taste of traditional Eastern sausage. It is the dish he puts his name behind.

sumobyarabic

He built the Arabic
vocabulary for sumo

Sumo had no Arabic audience because it had no Arabic explanation. The terms, the ranks, the rituals and the rules existed only in Japanese and English, so the sport reached the region as a punchline rather than a discipline.

Ramy built the missing layer himself. Under the tag #سومو_بالعربي and on the dedicated channel @sumobyarabic, he has been teaching sumo in Arabic for years, taking questions from viewers who have never seen a bout and translating the ranks, the rituals and the rules into language the region can follow.

"I started doing sumo in Arabic on social media, to explain sumo to Arab people, because they have this idea about sumo, that it is just fat guys. Sumo means to be more respectful."

It is the reason he is the name the Arab world knows in this sport, and the reason a federation or a brand entering sumo in the region starts with an audience that is already educated rather than one that has to be built from zero.

Facts

Ramy Elgazar, answered

Who is Ramy Elgazar?

Ramy Elgazar is an Egyptian American sumo wrestler, performer and chef based in Los Angeles. He is a four time US Sumo Open heavyweight champion, a ten time national champion of Egypt, and the first Egyptian and first Arab to win the heavyweight title at the US Sumo Open.

What else is Ramy Elgazar known as?

He also appears as Ramy El-Gazar, Ramy El Gazar and Sumo Guy, and in Arabic as رامي الجزار. All of them refer to the same person.

How many US Sumo Open titles has Ramy Elgazar won?

Four US Sumo Open heavyweight titles. The first came on 8 August 2015 at the Walter Pyramid in Long Beach, where he ended the eight year unbeaten streak of Byambajav Ulambayar, a four time World Sumo Champion.

Is Ramy Elgazar the first Arab to win the US Sumo Open?

Yes. He is the first Egyptian and the first Arab to win the heavyweight title at the US Sumo Open, the largest sumo tournament in the world outside Japan.

What is Ramy Elgazar's height and weight?

He is 1.93 m (6 ft 4 in) tall, with a peak competition weight of 260 kg.

Where is Ramy Elgazar from?

He grew up in El Mahalla El Kubra, a textile town in the Nile Delta, Egypt. He moved to Los Angeles in 2016 and is based there.

What sports did Ramy Elgazar compete in before sumo?

Greco Roman wrestling from the age of seven, then judo. He joined the Egyptian national judo team at fourteen and took the Arab Championship and an African bronze before turning seventeen. He has spent 34 years in combat sport.

What is Ramy Elgazar's role with the International Sumo Federation and USA Sumo?

He is an International Sumo Federation referee and officiated at the World Combat Games in Riyadh in 2023. He is a board member and assistant coach at USA Sumo and coach to the United States national sumo team.

Did Ramy Elgazar compete in the first professional sumo event in the UAE?

Yes. On 26 September 2026 he was the only Arab wrestler on the card at All Star Sumo at Space42 Arena in Abu Dhabi, the first international professional sumo competition held in the United Arab Emirates. It featured nine wrestlers and thirty six bouts and sold out.

How many live shows has Ramy Elgazar performed?

About 1,900 live shows worldwide since 2015, with a cumulative live audience of more than 9.5 million, across five continents.

How big is Ramy Elgazar's online audience?

Content he appears in has passed 1.5 billion views across 32 creator collaborations on 16 channels, including MrBeast, Nick DiGiovanni and Airrack. He has close to one million followers across platforms.

Has Ramy Elgazar appeared on television or in film?

Yes. His credits include Lip Sync Battle, Tengo Talento, Mucho Talento, The Project in Australia, Ozarks Live, and the films Hail Mary, The Grifter and The Wide West.
