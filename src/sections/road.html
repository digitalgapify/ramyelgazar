1992 to 2026

The road

1992

El Mahalla El Kubra

Starts Greco Roman wrestling at the Ghazl El Mahalla club at the age of seven, under Captain Abdel Hay El Qost, later head coach of the Saudi national team. His father is a maintenance technician at the Misr Spinning and Weaving Company and a former bodybuilder. The whole house is built around sport.

1994

Judo, and two sports at once

Takes up judo under Captain Metwally El Nafrawy. For two years he trains both disciplines in the same hall, wrestling from eight in the morning until noon, sleeping in the shade of the club, then judo from three until seven.

1999

Egypt national judo team at fourteen

Competes at over 100 kg. Takes the Arab Championship and an African bronze before turning seventeen. He fights the Arab final with a broken rib.

2000

The Sydney cycle

French expert Christophe Gagliano backs him for Olympic selection. He is not chosen. Egypt returns from Sydney without a judo medal. He leaves the sport soon afterwards.

2006

Sumo finds him

Introduced to sumo by Captain Bakr El Sharnouby. He enters the Egyptian national championship at the first attempt and wins it, still a junior.

2007 to 2012

Away from the mat

Five years out of competition. He keeps training, works, and waits for a door to open in a sport his country barely funds.

2013

International breakthrough

Joins Egypt's national sumo squad, then the strongest Arab team in the sport. He loses his first event in Poland by applying judo instinct, returns the same year and takes gold plus the Strongest Wrestler Cup. He stages the first public sumo event ever held in Egypt, at El Sawy Culturewheel in Cairo.

2014

Gold in Budapest, loss in Taiwan

Takes gold in Hungary. At the World Championships in Taiwan his foot sinks into newly laid clay and he loses to Byamba of Mongolia. What the Mongolian says to him afterwards is the reason he goes to America.

2015

America, and the title

He funds the trip himself out of savings, help from both parents and the sale of his motorcycle. He lands in New York on 17 July and works a nightclub door the same evening. Three weeks later he takes heavyweight gold and openweight silver at the US Sumo Open. A ninety minute American television documentary follows him through it. His following goes from five hundred to over one hundred thousand in days.

2016 to 2018

Turning professional

Signs with USA Sumo and moves to Los Angeles, where he has been based since. Bronze in 2016, a second gold in 2017. Begins the touring circuit, including Sumo and Sushi at the PlayStation Theater in New York in 2018. His photographs are exhibited in a Tokyo metro gallery under the title Egyptian Gallery in Tokyo.

2019 to 2020

Touring and filming

Openweight bronze at the US Sumo Open and a run of brand and creator work.

2021

The heaviest collision

Heavyweight silver. On the way there he meets 512 pound Simeon at 488 pounds himself, the heaviest meeting in more than two thousand matches of US Sumo Open history. He throws him down.

2023

Riyadh, and the officials' table

Officiates as an International Sumo Federation referee at the World Combat Games in Riyadh, and begins organising in the region.

2026

Abu Dhabi

The only Arab wrestler on the card at the first international professional sumo competition held in the UAE. Nine wrestlers, thirty six bouts, sold out at Space42 Arena, and the start of a development project for the sport in the Gulf.
