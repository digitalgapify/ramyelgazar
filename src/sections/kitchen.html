The second craft

Sumo Guy

He started cooking at seven or eight years old, before sumo entered the picture, in a house where the protein came off the roof. It was never a career plan. It was the thing he loved, and he kept doing it through every year of training.

The obsession turned technical. Competing at the top of the heavyweight division on a diet he designed and paid for himself, he learned nutrition the way he learned groundwork, by study. He now works as a sports nutritionist alongside the sport.

His first food video took half a million views in two hours. Two of his largest collaborations are food rather than sport, Nick DiGiovanni's whole cow cook and unlimited sushi challenge, sixty eight million views between them, plus a campaign for OXO knives and salt.

Everything he puts out is his own. His recipes are developed in his kitchen rather than adapted from anyone else, and the spice blends behind them are built by him and used across the range, which is what gives the food a signature rather than a style.

Sogo', Eastern style Egyptian sausage

Handmade from premium cuts and his own spice blend, chasing what he calls the true taste of traditional Eastern sausage. It is the dish he puts his name behind.
