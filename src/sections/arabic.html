He built the Arabic
vocabulary for sumo

Sumo had no Arabic audience because it had no Arabic explanation. The terms, the ranks, the rituals and the rules existed only in Japanese and English, so the sport reached the region as a punchline rather than a discipline.

Ramy built the missing layer himself. Under the tag #سومو_بالعربي and on the dedicated channel @sumobyarabic, he has been teaching sumo in Arabic for years, taking questions from viewers who have never seen a bout and translating the ranks, the rituals and the rules into language the region can follow.

"I started doing sumo in Arabic on social media, to explain sumo to Arab people, because they have this idea about sumo, that it is just fat guys. Sumo means to be more respectful."

It is the reason he is the name the Arab world knows in this sport, and the reason a federation or a brand entering sumo in the region starts with an audience that is already educated rather than one that has to be built from zero.
